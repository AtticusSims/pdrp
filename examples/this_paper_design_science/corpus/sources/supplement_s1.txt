## S1.1 Protocol Packaging, Corpus Traceability Key, and Tool-Interface Specification

The Progressive-Disclosure Research Protocol packages every research record into a three-layer Research Object Crate whose `ro-crate-metadata.json` conforms to the RO-Crate 1.1 JSON-LD profile (forward-compatible with the RO-Crate 1.3 community specification; Sefton *et al.* 2026):

- **Layer 0 (`COMPANION.md` + `ro-crate-metadata.json`):** Entry manifest strictly capped at `≤ 2,500` bytes (observed range across the four benchmark crates: `2,280–2,343` bytes, ~600 tokens). Includes a compact scope header, a verified **Negative-Coverage Map** enumerating absent data/analyses, and a flat one-line-per-card index grouped into five typed families (`C` Claims, `M` Methods/Protocols, `D` Data/Tables, `Q` Qualifications/Caveats, `U` Updates/Revisions). `ro-crate-metadata.json` records SHA-256 checksums for every immutable Layer-2 file.
- **Layer 1 (`cards/*.md`):** 16–18 atomic Markdown evidence cards per paper (68 cards total; 140–320 words each). YAML frontmatter encodes `source_id:start-end` line-range locators and directed edges from a six-relation vocabulary (`supported_by`, `qualified_by`, `contradicted_by`, `depends_on`, `superseded_by`, `answers_review`).
  - *Cycle 1 (`companion_v3`):* Invoking `read_card(card_id)` automatically traverses forward and reverse graph edges in software and appends up to three linked `Q`/`U` cards in the same tool response.
  - *Cycle 2 (`companion_v3_1`, protocol-only redesign over byte-identical corpora, cards, and questions):* (1) adds an explicit **Article-Sufficiency Gate** permitting direct answers without opening cards when a query is completely self-contained in the main article; (2) strips redundant YAML from `read_card()` payloads (30–34% smaller), caps automatic co-retrieval to the top-2 most relevant `Q`/`U` cards, and appends a deterministic `[VERIFY_PRIMARY_SPANS]` footer flagging short primary source spans (`≤ 80` lines) for `read_source()` verification; and (3) enforces a **Bounded Absence Confirmation** rule limiting negative-coverage re-checks to at most one targeted BM25 query.
- **Layer 2 (`corpora/sources/`):** Immutable primary article (`article.txt`) and all raw supplementary files (CSV tables, peer-review decision letters, author responses, checklists, and appendices), accessed via two flat tools shared identically between the raw-search and companion arms:
  - `search_corpus(query, scope="all"|"article"|"supplementary", top_k=5)`: Okapi BM25 (*k*₁ = 1.5, *b* = 0.75) over overlapping line windows (zero-hit rate: `31 / 2,010 = 1.5%`, vs. ~66% under naive substring lookup in preliminary prototyping).
  - `read_source(source_id, start_line, end_line)`: Direct bounded inspection (up to 150 lines per call), including an `"article"` alias.

## S1.2 Worked Artifact Specimen: *Hippocampal Ripples (eLife)* (`elife-111304`)

To make the designed artifact concrete, below are verbatim excerpts from the frozen open-access companion crate for Liu, Jing *et al.* (2026, *eLife* 15:RP111304, CC-BY-4.0), illustrating Layer 0, Layer 1, software-enforced qualification co-retrieval, and Cycle 2's verification cue.

### Excerpt A — Layer-0 Entry Manifest (`COMPANION.md`, 2,319 bytes total)

```markdown
# Companion: Ramping-up hippocampal ripples and their neocortical coupling support human visual short-term memory
DOI: 10.7554/eLife.111304 | VOR: 2026-11-20 | CC-BY-4.0 | 3 reviewed preprints -> VOR v1

## Coverage Map
- **Only in `author_response`:** 4 omitted controls (1/f slope, ripple-only trials, probe-lock, LTM split), GLMM formulas, HFA beta=0.002, 0-1s encoding p-values, preprint "solid" assessment, revision log.
- **Only in `supp1` & `mdar`:** Per-patient HPC/LTL/AMY/PHC/ERC channels, HPC slopes, trials (N=3,444); MDAR: Liu 2020 reanalysis, no preregistration, power calc, or blinding.
- **Only in `elife_assessment_and_versions` & `public_reviews`:** "solid"->"convincing" upgrade, editors, Reviewers #1-#3 VOR caveats (HFA, AMY+ERC, LTM, >1s semantics).
- **Absent:** Raw iEEG traces, trial decoding matrices, OSF files/URL, healthy controls, load/delay/lure manipulations, saccades, per-patient age/sex/meds/SOZ/hemisphere, LTL slopes.

## Card Index (excerpt of 16 total)
- C02 [claim] HPC ramping predicts VSTM vs LTL | ramping, GLMM, accuracy, gray-matter
- M02 [method] LMM/GLMM specs & trial rules | Poisson, lme4, emmeans, 5000-perms
- Q01 [qualification] Epilepsy cohort, HFA & MTL gaps | HFA, p=0.662, AMY, ERC
- U01 [update] Preprint->VOR & assessment upgrade | versions, solid, convincing
```

### Excerpt B — Layer-1 Claim Card (`C02`) Triggering Software Co-Retrieval of Qualification Card (`Q01`)

When an AI assistant invokes `read_card("C02")`, the retrieval server reads `C02`'s directed link metadata (`source_locators: [article:98-122, supp1:11-26, author_response:139-156, author_response:252-255]`, `linked_cards: [Q01, Q02, Q03]`), strips redundant YAML headers in Cycle 2, and **automatically bundles `Q01`** (which carries an inbound `qualifies -> C02` edge) into the same tool response:

**Requested Card `C02` (condensed excerpt):** Across the 7 s maintenance delay, an all-trial Poisson GLMM reveals progressive ramping-up of HPC ripple counts (`β=0.012, z=4.678, p<0.001`; `article:101`) with a significant `time × accuracy` interaction (`β=0.018, z=2.171, p=0.030`), driven by remembered trials (`β=0.014, p_FDR<0.001`) and absent on forgotten trials (`β=−0.002, p_FDR=0.778`; `article:103`). A three-way `time × accuracy × region` GLMM confirms dissociation from LTL (`β=0.053, p=0.044`; `article:122`, `author_response:154-156`).

**Auto-Bundled Qualification Card `Q01` (software forcing function, excerpt):**

1. *Clinical neurosurgical sample without healthy controls or design safeguards:* All 13 participants are drug-resistant epilepsy patients reanalyzed from Liu et al. (2020a) with no healthy controls, preregistration, power calculation, or blinding (`mdar:102-104, 148-150`).
2. *Unresolved distinction between sharp-wave ripples and broadband HFA:* Continuous 70–180 Hz HFA shows a marginal maintenance ramp (`β=0.002, p=0.074`) and **does not differ significantly in slope from detected HPC ripples (`p=0.662`; `article:101`, `author_response:200`)**; Reviewer #2 characterizes the VOR as showing *"solid evidence that hippocampal high-frequency activity ramps up"* (`public_reviews:29`).
3. *Declined non-hippocampal MTL control:* Authors declined Reviewer #3's amygdala/entorhinal control due to sparse electrode sampling (`supp1:11-26`, `author_response:206-209`).

**Cycle 2 Deterministic Footer:** `[VERIFY_PRIMARY_SPANS] Short primary spans linked (≤80 lines): supp1:11-26, author_response:139-156, public_reviews:29-41. Call read_source() before quoting verbatim wording or exact table cells.`
