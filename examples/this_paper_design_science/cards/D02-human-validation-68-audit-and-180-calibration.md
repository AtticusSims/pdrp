# D02: Complete 68/68 Author Crate Audit and 180-item Blinded Judge Calibration dataset and reliability CIs

Task 1 (Author Crate Audit) logged all 68 of 68 cards (100% completion across all four studies; article:270, human_validation_report:3-10, supplement_s1:146-160): 64/68 (94.1%) approved as-is, 4/68 (5.9%) minor precision edits (digital-creativity C01, elife-111304 C02 paired slope-difference phrasing, elife-110672 Q02 ~5% conversion wording and Q04 counting typo), and 0/68 (0.0%) substantive factual errors, plus rejection of one DeepSeek V3.2 audit false positive on digital-creativity C02. Stopwatch logs on the two authored internal manuscripts recorded 17.4 min (Computational Aesthetics, 18 cards) and 12.6 min (Creative-Coding Review, 16 cards), averaging 15.0 min/paper; external eLife papers were audited as a non-author reader without engaging the stopwatch (self-reported ~15 min each). Task 2 (Blinded Calibration, n = 180; Table 8, article:274-289, supplement_s1:161-179) yielded: AI-Unanimous stratum (n = 90, 92.3% share): 93.3% exact agreement, kappa = 0.834 (95% CI 0.683-0.948, almost perfect; pass precision 95.4%); AI-Disagreement stratum (n = 90, 7.7% share): human passed 82.2% (confirming conservative consensus). Post-stratified population projections across N = 2,304 (5,000-draw stratified bootstrap CIs): 2-Judge Consensus 87.4% agree (82.2-91.9%), kappa = 0.710 (0.588-0.813), bias -6.5 pp; Judge A (GLM-5.3) 91.6% agree (86.4-96.0%), kappa = 0.794 (0.661-0.900, substantial bordering almost perfect), bias -0.3 pp; Judge B (Kimi-K2.5) 88.2% agree (83.1-92.5%), kappa = 0.722 (0.603-0.825), bias -5.0 pp. Two-sided Fisher exact bias tests (72 companion vs. 46 raw) were non-significant across all judges and directions (Consensus p = 1.00 / 0.35; Judge A p = 1.00 / 1.00; Judge B p = 0.24 / 0.18).
