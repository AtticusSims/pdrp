# C04: Cycle 2 procedural rules over byte-identical cards halve compression substitution and lift reasoning cloud models

Holding all four frozen corpora, all 68 evidence cards, every entry manifest, and all 64 card-blind questions 100% byte-identical between Cycle 1 and Cycle 2 isolated the causal impact of three procedural interface rules: the Article-Sufficiency Gate, compact YAML-free payloads with deterministic [VERIFY_PRIMARY_SPANS] short-span cues (<=80 lines), and Bounded Absence Confirmation (article:118-124, 210-234). Cycle 2 nearly halved compression-substitution losses (card-only premature stopping without opening brief peer-review letters), restored Inkling 2 Small to parity (85.9% -> 92.2%), lifted Grok 4.6 (84.4% -> 89.1%), Qwen 3.8 Max (93.8% -> 95.3%), Mistral Large 4 (89.1% -> 92.2%), and local Gemma 4 31B (46.9% -> 54.7%), and reduced paired tokens significantly vs. Cycle 1 (p < 0.0001) and full-card preloading (58,253 vs. 71,892, p < 10^-14). Across the four mandatory-reasoning cloud models (n = 256), Cycle 2 reached 92.2% strict consensus clean success with 0/256 hallucinations (vs. 88.3% raw BM25, +3.9 pp, p = 0.0309; and 93.0% vs. 85.2% preload, +7.8 pp, p = 0.0129; article:214-224). In a post-hoc unified five-cloud reasoning-ON pool adding ablation DeepSeek V3.2 ON (n = 320), Cycle 2 beat raw BM25 by +5.6 pp (88.4% vs. 82.8%, p = 0.0014; Holm p_adj = 0.010; Judge A +4.4 pp, p = 0.0161; Judge B +5.3 pp, p = 0.0033; question-clustered p = 0.0024) while using 17% fewer median tokens (86,510 vs. 104,116, p = 0.0003) at exact hallucination parity (1/320 each; supplement_s1:180-191). See Q01 and Q04 for Frontier-4 single-judge ceiling and role-overlap checks.
