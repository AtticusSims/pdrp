# U01: Peer-review dispositions tempering GLORI, isoform, non-DRACH, and cell-type claims

The manuscript underwent four reviewed preprint stages (dates/texts not archived in this corpus) before publication as Version of Record v1 on 2026-11-20 under Reviewing Editor Pedro Batista and Senior Editor Yamini Dalal. Per the Editors' note (`public_reviews:9`), the final VOR was assessed directly by the Reviewing Editor without sending back to the original two reviewers.

Across revision rounds, the authors executed substantive analytical and rhetorical changes captured in `public_reviews` and `author_response`: (1) performed CellTiter-Glo viability assays (10 replicate wells/condition) confirming 30 µM STM2457 caused no toxicity; (2) repeated DAVID KEGG enrichment using an expression-matched ~13,000-gene background; (3) added three supplemental isoform loci (`KRTAP1-5`, `HSPA8`, `CITED2`) and replaced broad Dorado 'outperforms' GLORI claims with RNA-variant assignment wording (`article:248`); (4) added two GLORI coverage and stoichiometry breakdown subplots (referred to as Fig S21 in `author_response:21`, published as Fig 3—figure supplement 1D–E) and retracted claims that GLORI-only calls are 'largely spurious' or that GLORI 'exhibits reduced specificity'; (5) clarified Fig 3D's GLORI-recovery directionality; (6) cited Zhang et al. (DOI: `10.1126/sciadv.ads4750`) supporting non-DRACH METTL3–METTL14 activity for `GGAUU`; and (7) removed 'finely tuned' neuronal regulation claims while changing Abstract and Results headings to 'fibroblast and neuronal datasets' (declining Reviewer #2's optional suggestion to change the paper title).
