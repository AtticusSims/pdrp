# M01: Cell models, STM2457 perturbation, viability assay, and matched-input RNA

Two human cell models were profiled: (1) primary normal human dermal fibroblasts (NHDFs; Lonza Bioscience, anonymous donors, validated, used for <50 population doublings per MDAR) cultured in DMEM with 5% heat-inactivated FBS and 1% penicillin-streptomycin; and (2) v-myc-immortalized human fetal dorsal root ganglion HD10.6 cells (gift from Anna Cliffe, University of Virginia; original isolation PMID: 10377351; confirmed human female by STR profiling, PMID: 41415445). Both tested negative for mycoplasma routinely.

Undifferentiated HD10.6 cells were maintained on 17 µg/mL fibronectin in Advanced DMEM/F12 with 1× Neurocult SM1, 2 mM L-glutamine, 1× Primocin, 10 ng/mL prostaglandin E1, and fresh 0.5 ng/mL bFGF. For maturation, cells were seeded at 25,000 cells/cm² on poly-L-ornithine (50 µg/mL in 0.5 M borate buffer, pH 8.5) and 1 µg/mL fibronectin, then switched to Neurobasal medium with SM1, L-glutamine, Primocin, 1 µg/mL doxycycline, 50 ng/mL NGF, and 25 ng/mL each of CNTF, GDNF, and NT-3 (matured 10 days; complete neuronal morphology reported at 12 days).

Cells received 30 µM STM2457 (Sigma) or DMSO vehicle for 48 hr. CellTiter-Glo viability assays (Promega; 96-well plates, 10 replicate wells/condition, EnVision 2103 reader, unpaired two-tailed Student's t-test) showed no viability effect. TRIzol-isolated total RNA was split 1/3 for DRS and 2/3 for GLORI, followed by Dynabeads poly(A) selection; matched transcriptome-wide IVT RNA was synthesized from cDNA primed with a poly(A) primer and T7-incorporating strand-switch primer.
