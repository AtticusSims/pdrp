# D01: Patient cohort, DMS trial imbalance, and MDAR reporting disclosures

This study reanalyzes intracranial EEG recordings from Liu et al. (2020a) (`article:227`, `mdar:102-104`). Thirteen neurosurgical patients with drug-resistant epilepsy (mean age 26.77±5.48 years, 7 females, normal/corrected vision) who had simultaneous HPC and LTL depth electrodes were recorded at the Center of Epileptology, Xuanwu Hospital, Capital Medical University, Beijing, China under Xuanwu Hospital IRB approval with written informed consent (`article:47, 227, 319`). Per-patient demographics (individual ages, sex assignments, ethnicity, anti-seizure medications, seizure-onset zones, and hemispheres) are not reported.

Stimuli comprised 56 everyday object pictures (4 categories × 14 images: animals, fruits, electrical devices, furniture) paired with 112 two-character Chinese verbs (each picture paired with two verbs across consecutive runs). Each DMS run presented 14 unique pairs × 3 repetitions (42 trials/run): 300 ms fixation, 800–1200 ms jittered blank, 3 s word–picture encoding, 7 s picture-absent maintenance (cue word remained visible), and ≤2 s match/nonmatch retrieval (50% target, 50% highly similar lure; `article:233-235`). Behavioral accuracy averaged 89.71±4.26% with RT 1.03±0.22 s, and remembered RTs were shorter than forgotten RTs (`t(12)=–5.88, p<0.001`; `article:47`).

Participant-level trial counts (`supp1:28-42`) reveal strong class imbalance across 3,444 total VSTM trials (4–8 runs; 168–336 trials/patient): 3,082 remembered (149–309/patient, mean 237.08±49.81) versus 362 forgotten (10–71/patient, mean 27.85±15.63; Sub13 has only 10 forgotten trials, Sub8 16, Sub12 17). Note a minor reporting discrepancy: `article:235` lists 6.14±1.46 runs and 258.00±61.32 trials/patient, whereas summing the 13 patients in `supp1:28-42` gives 6.31 runs and 264.92±57.85 trials. The MDAR checklist (`mdar:95-211`) confirms no nonhuman animals, no preregistration or trial ID, **sample size determination = not done**, and **blinding = not done**; `article:323-325` Data Availability is empty in the extracted corpus despite Reviewer #2 praising OSF data/code (`author_response:55`).
