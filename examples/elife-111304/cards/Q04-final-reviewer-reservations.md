# Q04: Final VOR public review dispositions and remaining reservations (Reviewers #1-#3)

Although the VOR eLife Assessment upgraded evidence strength to "convincing", it explicitly noted that "some reviewers had concerns about interpretation of the ripple activity" (`elife_assessment_and_versions:10`). Final public reviews (`public_reviews:6-59`) split sharply across the three reviewers:

- **Reviewer #1 (`sa1`, `public_reviews:6-23`):** Judged the results "compelling novel evidence" and stated they are fully satisfied with the raw IPI validation, ±50 ms vs. ±0.5 s disambiguation, and figure revisions, with no further comments.
- **Reviewer #2 (`sa2`, `public_reviews:26-29`):** Praised the authors' responsiveness (ripple validation, aperiodic 1/f and HFA controls, direct interaction tests, peak-lag analyses), concluding the revised paper provides "solid evidence that hippocampal high-frequency activity ramps up during maintenance in a manner that scales with short-term memory success," while maintaining three open questions: (1) ripple specificity vs. broadband HFA, (2) specificity to short-term vs. long-term memory, and (3) whether coupled ripples actually link the hippocampal ramp to cortical reactivation.
- **Reviewer #3 (`sa3`, `public_reviews:32-59`):** Welcomed the participant-level swarm plot and viewed the `z=1.8` (`p=0.071`) three-way VSTM vs. LTM trend as meaningful at `N=13`, but recorded five unresolved criticisms: (1) disputed the Discussion claim that LTM ripples act post-encoding rather than during encoding, citing Henin et al. (2021) and Sakon et al. (2024) and calling Norman et al. (2019) post-encoding ripples an unreplicated statistical fluke; (2) maintained that an AMY or pooled AMY+ERC control should have been run despite lower electrode counts; (3) objected to overlaying multiple channels per trial row in Fig 2—fig supp 3 rasters and recommended replacing Figs 2b–e with Fig 2—fig supp 4's tuned 0–1 s encoding window; (4) doubted that semantic reinstatement requires >1 s (1.85–2.25 s training window) and noted encoding decoding did not differ from maintenance (`ps_cluster>0.800`), questioning large Fig 5d error bars; and (5) noted it remains puzzling that coupled ripples fail to ramp when HPC ripples do.
