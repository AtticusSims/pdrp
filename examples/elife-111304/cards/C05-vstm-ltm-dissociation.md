# C05: VSTM ripple dynamics dissociate from subsequent LTM cued recall

Because each DMS trial pairs a two-character Chinese verb cue with an object image for a later cued-recall test, the authors tested whether VSTM-maintenance ripple signatures merely reflect concurrent LTM encoding (`article:47, 180`). After each VSTM run, patients completed a 1 min mathematical countback task and a 1–4 min rest before LTM cued recall (14 unique pairs/run, mean 88.31±19.28 LTM trials/patient). LTM trials counted as "remembered" only when a self-paced subjective "remember" keypress was verified by a correct 4-AFC category response within 3 s (`article:237`).

Splitting VSTM-remembered trials by subsequent LTM outcome reveals that HPC ripple ramping during maintenance is significant for BOTH subsequently LTM-remembered (`β=0.017, z=4.141, p_FDR<0.001`) and LTM-forgotten trials (`β=0.011, z=2.987, p_FDR=0.003`), with no `time × LTM accuracy` difference (`β=0.003, z=0.439, p=0.661`; `article:118, 180`). Likewise, neither HPC–LTL coupled ripple rates (`ps_cluster>0.626`) nor coupled ripple-locked LTL reactivation (`ps_cluster>0.135`) differ by subsequent LTM outcome (`article:118, 180`). During the later LTM test itself, however, HPC ripple rates replicate canonical LTM retrieval effects, exceeding forgotten trials 1600–1900 ms pre-response (`p_cluster=0.047`; `article:73, 93`).

Two revision analyses bound this dissociation:
1. **Direct three-way interaction (`time × memory × test_type` [VSTM vs. LTM]):** Added per Reviewer #3, the interaction reaches only a non-significant trend (`β=0.039, z=1.809, p=0.071`; `article:180`, `author_response:238-246`), falling short of a formal `p<0.05` double dissociation.
2. **Behavioral LTM stratification (in `author_response:225-230` only):** Median-splitting participants on overall LTM accuracy (median 0.45; Poor LTM `n=7` vs. Good LTM `n=6`) shows no significant VSTM accuracy difference (`t(11)=–1.20, p=0.254`). Authors note intervening countback, rest, and retrieval factors could also mask delay-to-LTM correlations (`article:208`).
