# Q02: Dynamic-coding paradox, zero directional lag, and impure VSTM task limits

Three mechanistic and experimental limitations highlighted by Reviewers #2 and #3 and acknowledged in the VOR Discussion constrain causal and theoretical conclusions:

1. **Dynamic-coding ramping paradox:** The dynamic coding framework predicts ramping reactivation bursts in the region storing content (`article:33`). Yet LTL ripples do NOT ramp (and *decrease* significantly on remembered trials in the 89 gray-matter channels, `β=–0.009, p_FDR=0.017`; `article:113`), HPC–LTL coupled ripples do NOT ramp (`ps_FDR>0.281`, `time × memory p=0.506`; `article:138, 147`), and coupled ripple-locked LTL decoding does NOT ramp (`p=0.586`; `article:138, 174`). Coupled LTL ripples show only a non-significant upward trend (`β=0.032, p=0.126`) vs. declining uncoupled LTL ripples (`β=–0.014, p=0.026`; interaction `p=0.088`; `article:143`). Both Reviewers #2 and #3 note this leaves the link between HPC ramping and coupled-ripple cortical reactivation unresolved (`public_reviews:29, 59`).
2. **Absence of directional coupling:** Peak-to-peak lag tests show no systematic HPC precedence over LTL ripples in any task stage (all trials `ps_FDR>0.126`; remembered `ps_FDR>0.069`; `article:128, 145`). Accordingly, the authors removed causal "drive" wording (`author_response:124`) and leave open whether HPC ripples gate cortical reactivation or co-occur via shared/reciprocal inputs (`article:196`).
3. **Impure VSTM task and absent parametric/oculomotor controls:** Trials combine familiar, semantically rich everyday pictures, explicit Chinese-verb cue–picture associative learning (with the cue word visible throughout maintenance), a fixed single-item 7 s delay, and subsequent LTM testing (`article:202-204, 235`). No parametric manipulations of memory load, delay duration, or lure similarity were included (`article:208`, `author_response:219-223`), and **no eye-tracking or saccade controls** are reported. Moreover, the direct VSTM vs. LTM three-way interaction is only marginal (`β=0.039, z=1.809, p=0.071`; `article:180`).
