# U01: Preprint-to-VOR timeline and eLife Assessment upgrade from solid to convincing

Published as a Version of Record (VOR, version 1) on `2026-11-20` (`DOI: 10.7554/eLife.111304`, CC-BY-4.0), the manuscript progressed through three reviewed preprint entries (Entries 1–3, `status=preprint`) before VOR publication (Entry 4, `status=vor`, `stage=published`) under Reviewing Editor Anna C Schapiro and Senior Editor Huan Luo (`elife_assessment_and_versions:3-14`).

The editorial **eLife Assessment** underwent a concrete strength-of-evidence upgrade between the original preprint reviews and the published VOR:
- **Preprint v1 Assessment (`author_response:7-9`):** Rated the study **"important"** with **"solid"** novel evidence, praising the intracranial electrophysiology, multivariate classifiers, and experimental design, but flagging that *"several aspects of the theoretical framing, mechanistic interpretation, and analysis strategy are incomplete."*
- **Final VOR Assessment (`elife_assessment_and_versions:9-10`):** Retained **"important"** significance and upgraded strength of evidence from **"solid"** to **"convincing"**, replacing the broad incompleteness critique with the targeted caveat *"though some reviewers had concerns about interpretation of the ripple activity."*

As summarized in `author_response:11-17`, three revision pillars drove this upgrade: (1) reorganizing the Introduction and Discussion to motivate hippocampal ripples in online VSTM without framing VSTM/LTM as rigid separate systems, dropping causal claims that HPC ripples "drive" LTL reactivation in favor of synchronous/reciprocal coupling, and situating the late-encoding (1.85–2.25 s) reactivation within representational transformation; (2) adding rigorous signal validations (raw IPI, aperiodic 1/f slope, 70–180 Hz HFA comparison), formal multi-way interaction models, peak-lag tests, and maintenance-trajectory analyses of coupled ripples and decoding; and (3) expanding Methods (LTM cued-recall protocol, neurologist IED screening, trial-inclusion criteria, 1,000× bootstrapping) alongside clearer group figures and participant-level tables.
