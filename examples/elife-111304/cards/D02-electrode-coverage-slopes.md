# D02: Participant-level electrode coverage across HPC, LTL, and unanalyzed MTL sites

Supplementary file 1 Table A (`supp1:11-26`) details the exact bipolar channel distribution across all 13 patients (`Sub1`–`Sub13`), revealing marked inter-patient imbalance in anatomical sampling:
- **Hippocampus (HPC):** 69 channels across all 13 patients (mean 5.31±5.01, range 1–18). Sub4 and Sub12 contribute only 1 HPC channel each; Sub1, Sub3, Sub5, and Sub6 have 2 each; Sub2 and Sub10 have 4; Sub8 has 6; Sub9 has 7; Sub7 has 8; Sub13 has 12; and Sub11 has 18 (`article:58`, `supp1:12-26`).
- **Lateral Temporal Lobe (LTL):** 132 channels across all 13 patients (mean 10.15±7.82, range 2–22: Sub6/Sub7/Sub10 have 2 each; Sub5 has 3; Sub11 has 5; Sub4/Sub9 have 7; Sub13 has 11; Sub12 has 12; Sub2 has 16; Sub8 has 21; Sub1/Sub3 have 22; `article:91`, `supp1:12-26`). A gray-matter control analysis retains `n=89` LTL bipolar pairs with ≥1 contact in or within 2 mm of LTL gray matter (`article:113, 122`).
- **Sparse unanalyzed non-HPC MTL sites (`supp1:11-26`, `author_response:206-209`):** Amygdala (AMY) = 20 channels across 9 patients (range 1–5), parahippocampal cortex (PHC) = 6 channels across 5 patients (range 1–2), and entorhinal cortex (ERC) = 2 channels in 1 patient (Sub5). No frontal, parietal, or primary visual/occipital cortical areas were evaluated (`article:198, 200`).

Participant-level HPC ramping slopes (`supp1:12-25`) are positive on remembered trials in 11/13 patients (range –0.134 to 0.503; negative in Sub1 –0.134, Sub12 –0.061) and positive on forgotten trials in 4/13 (Sub1 0.005, Sub5 0.147, Sub6 0.115, Sub8 0.228; range –0.257 to 0.228). Remembered slopes exceed forgotten slopes in 11/13 patients (exceptions: Sub1, Sub8). **Corpus discrepancy:** `author_response:374-376` states participant-level LTL ramping slopes were added alongside LTL channel counts in Table S1, but `supp1:11-26` contains LTL channel counts only and omits any per-patient LTL β column.
